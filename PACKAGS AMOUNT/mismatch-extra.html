🟣 Amount Mismatch — Extra Payment

Sale (paid) amount, Billing (bill) amount se zyada hai — customer ne package se zyada payment ki hai.

Abhi tak koi verification nahi chali. Pehle Dual Data Import page se Sale aur Billing files upload karein, phir yahan "Run / Refresh Verification" dabayein.
🟣 Ye woh bills hain jahan Sale se mili payment, Billing amount se zyada hai. Extra Amount column dikhata hai kitna zyada mila hai. Agar us user ka koi purana Pending bill hai, to Extra Amount usay (FIFO — sabse purana pehle) khud-b-khud (automatically) clear kar deta hai — jaise Bill Rs. 2000 + Extra Rs. 4000 aur ek purana Pending Rs. 2000 ho, to wo pending automatic clear ho jayega aur Rs. 2000 extra bacha rahega, koi click ki zaroorat nahi. Agar koi pending cover nahi hota, row waisi hi rahegi. Kisi row ko auto-clear se nikalna ho to "Undo" button se alag rakh sakte hain. Billing Amount aur Paid Amount columns bhi edit ki ja sakti hain — sahi amount likh kar 💾 Save dabayein, dono barabar ho jayein to row automatically Clear ho jayegi.
